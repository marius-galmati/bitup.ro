## How you ask for help

Eyebrow: `Three ways`
**H2:** Three ways, the same system

Three cards:

**WhatsApp, with Cipri**
Write in your own words, as you would to anyone: "my VPN isn't working", "I can't open the file". Cipri recognizes you by number, asks two or three questions, suggests steps. If that doesn't fix it, it opens the ticket itself and a person takes over. The status comes back on WhatsApp too.
Number: `+40 750 263 653` (link to the same `wa.me`)

**Form on helpdesk.bitup.ro**
For anyone who prefers to write more or attach more files. You open the ticket and get the confirmation and statuses over email.
Link: helpdesk.bitup.ro

**Phone, for emergencies**
When nobody's email works, the server is down, or you've received a ransom note. Not for "my printer isn't working".
Number: `+40 757 150 894` (`tel:` link, `data-event="tel_click"`) · Hours: Monday–Friday, 8:00–18:00; outside them, emergencies only

## What counts as an emergency

Eyebrow: `Priorities`
**H2:** So we both know what to expect

Simple table (3 rows):

| How serious | Examples | What I do |
|---|---|---|
| **Critical** | Nobody can work: the server is down, email is down for everyone, suspected attack, encrypted files | I respond immediately, including outside business hours, on the emergency number. Priority over everything. |
| **Important** | Part of the company can't work: a department without access, a critical app down, the laptop of someone everything depends on | I respond during business hours, in under two hours by contract (actual average: under 30 minutes), and usually fix it that business day. |
| **Normal** | One person, one problem: forgotten password, printer, a program to install, a question | Cipri answers right away; what it can't fix, I fix in order of arrival, during business hours. |

Line under the table: The exact times are in your contract. What it says there beats what it says here.

## What happens after you write

Steps (4), the same sequence as on How we work, shorter:

**1.** You write on WhatsApp or on the helpdesk.
**2.** Cipri answers right away and tries to walk you through a fix.
**3.** If that doesn't work, the ticket is opened automatically and I take it over. You get a confirmation.
**4.** When it's solved, you get the status on the same channel. At the end of the month, everything that happened is in the report.

## Tips that make things faster

Eyebrow: `Help me help you`
**H2:** Three things that shorten any ticket

List:
- **A screenshot of the error.** Worth more than three paragraphs. Cipri picks it up straight from WhatsApp.
- **What you were doing when it happened.** "I clicked the link in the email" or "I turned on the laptop this morning" completely changes the diagnosis.
- **Whether anyone else has the same problem.** One person or everyone? That's the difference between a laptop and a server.

## What Cipri doesn't do

Eyebrow: `To be clear`
**H2:** What the assistant doesn't do

Text (70ch): Cipri doesn't go into your computer, change your passwords, run commands, or decide on its own that a problem is closed when you say it isn't. It's the first line: it listens, asks, suggests, takes notes. Anything the steps don't solve goes to a person, with their name on the ticket. To skip it, write "I want a ticket" and you go straight to the next step.
